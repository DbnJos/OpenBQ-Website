    Why biometric quality matters

    Poor biometric quality is a governance issue.

    Digital identity systems succeed or fail on data quality.

    Poor capture leads to failed enrolments, manual exceptions and exclusion — especially for vulnerable populations. These problems often surface after deployment, when fixes are expensive and politically sensitive.

    Without quality checks

    • Enrolments fail later
    • Manual exception queues grow
    • People are excluded from services
    • Vendor claims are hard to verify

    With OpenBq

    • Re-capture on the spot
    • Fewer exceptions
    • More inclusive enrolment
    • Evidence for policy and procurement
