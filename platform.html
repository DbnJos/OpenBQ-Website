      Built to scale with your programme

      From a single pilot to a national identity programme, OpenBq is designed to grow with your data and adapt to changing demand.

      Start small

      Run on a single machine for pilots, lab studies and smaller datasets, with no complex infrastructure needed.

      Grow to national scale

      Add computing resources as datasets grow, so assessing millions of records stays practical.

      Flex with demand

      Scale resources up for peaks such as enrolment drives or audits, and back down when demand falls.

      Extensible by design: add new quality engines and modalities through bqcore as your needs change.

      Designed for existing ecosystems

      Quality assessment where it matters

      OpenBq works in two operational contexts: at the point of capture, and on data that is already enrolled.

      It integrates with MOSIP’s Compliance Toolkit and national ID workflows, alongside your existing vendors.

      Standards & algorithms

      Built on internationally recognised standards

      OpenBq utilises benchmarked quality algorithms to support analysis in compliance with the ISO/IEC 29794 suite and other quality algorithms — and highlights open-source engines such as NFIQ 2.0 and OFIQ to adopt or contribute to.
