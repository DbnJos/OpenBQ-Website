FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Talk to the team.

Do we need to replace our current biometric vendors?

No. OpenBq is vendor agnostic and works alongside existing systems and suppliers — it measures quality, it doesn’t replace capture or matching.

Which standards and algorithms are supported?

OpenBq supports analysis in line with the ISO/IEC 29794 suite and best-practice open-source algorithms such as NFIQ 2.0 (fingerprint) and OFIQ (face), and can integrate custom engines.

How can we run it?

Through bqconnect: a command line interface or a desktop GUI. It can run as a single node (scale up) or as a coordinator with many worker nodes (scale out), for example on Kubernetes.

How does it work with MOSIP?

OpenBq integrates with MOSIP’s Compliance Toolkit and national ID workflows, and can provide quality feedback at the registration client.

Is it free? What are the optional services?

Yes — OpenBq is licensed under Apache 2.0. Optional services such as report hosting, training and lab validation are available to support adoption and fund long-term development.
